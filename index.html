About

Where healthcare operations meets data science

I'm a Healthcare Data Analyst with over 5 years of experience transforming complex healthcare and revenue cycle data into decisions hospital leadership can act on. My background in Pharmacy & Biotechnology gives me something most analysts don't have: a working understanding of clinical and pricing operations before the data ever reaches a dashboard.

Across King's College Hospital Dubai, Saudi German Health, and Andalusia Group, I've built the reporting layer that finance and operations teams rely on daily — from Revenue Cycle Management KPIs and denial analytics to SBS/NAPHIES-aligned pricing models and SFDA-compliant price lists. I specialize in taking a messy, multi-source healthcare dataset and turning it into a Power BI dashboard an executive can read in thirty seconds.

Revenue Cycle Management Dashboard

Business Problem
Finance leadership lacked a single, real-time view of claims, denials, and collections across payers, causing delayed intervention on revenue leakage.
Solution
Built a Power BI model on SQL Server data with DAX measures for clean claim rate, denial rate, and AR aging buckets, refreshed daily.
Impact
Gave leadership same-day visibility into revenue cycle performance and faster denial follow-up.
Technology
Power BI · SQL Server · DAX

Surgery Performance Dashboard

Business Problem
Surgery scheduling delays between booking, insurance authorization, and completion were invisible until they affected patients.
Solution
Mapped the full patient journey in Power BI, flagging stage-by-stage delays and turnaround time against target SLAs.
Impact
20% improvement in operational visibility and a 15% faster average turnaround time.
Technology
Power BI · Power Query
